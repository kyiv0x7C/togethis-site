Question cards for conversations that bring you closer

Togethis is a Ukrainian-made iOS app of conversation question cards for couples, families and friends: a free starter deck, paid decks as one-time purchases with no subscription, no ads and no accounts, in Ukrainian and English.

How it works

What it is

Togethis is decks of question cards you pull up on your phone at the table, on a date or in a video call. Swipe for the next question; who answers first is up to you. The starter deck “For Friends” with 50 questions is free. The paid decks — “For Couples”, “For Family”, “Party Night” — are bought once and stay yours forever.

Who it is for

Couples

Deeper than “how was your day?”

The “For Couples” deck: from light and romantic questions to the most honest ones. Guess mode — who knows whom better. A memories journal for the answers worth keeping. A weekly streak for two.

Family

The evening everyone is at the table

The “For Family” deck — 30 questions that bring three generations together: from funny memories to stories you have never heard. The first 6 cards are free.

Friends

A game with no props

The free starter deck “For Friends” — 50 questions. The “Party Night” deck — 30 questions with stories, confessions and a bit of healthy absurdity, plus action cards that wake up any party.

How it works

  1. Pick a deck

    Couples, family or friends. Or “Tonight's Mix” — a cocktail from all the decks you own.

  2. Swipe the cards

    One question per screen. Read it aloud, let everyone answer, swipe on. Action cards change the rhythm now and then.

  3. Come back daily

    Question of the Day and a shared Question of the Week, bonus cards for finished decks, a memories journal with your best answers.

Sample questions from the starter deck

Eight of the 50 free questions in the “For Friends” deck, exactly as they appear in the app:

Privacy

Togethis has no accounts, sign-up, ads or advertising identifiers. Deck progress and the memories journal stay on your phone. The internet is used for three things: downloading questions, verifying purchases with the App Store, and sending anonymous usage statistics without personal data. Details are in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Is Togethis a subscription?

No. The download is free, the 50-question starter deck “For Friends” is free, and paid decks are one-time purchases that stay yours forever. Two bundles exist: All Access (every current deck) and Lifetime (every deck, forever). There is no subscription in the app.

Do I need an account?

No. Togethis has no accounts, sign-up, ads or tracking. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored with the Restore Purchases button. Family Sharing is supported.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Downloaded decks work without an internet connection. The network is only used to download new questions and to verify purchases with the App Store.

Which languages are the questions in?

English and Ukrainian. The interface and card language switch in the app settings. Ukraine is the primary market, so every question exists in Ukrainian as well.

How is Togethis different from Paired or Agapé?

Paired and Agapé are subscription apps for couples built around daily programs. Togethis sells decks of questions as one-time purchases, needs no subscription or account, works in English and Ukrainian, and is not only for couples: there are decks for family dinners and for groups of friends.

Is there an Android version?

Togethis is currently available for iPhone on the App Store only. One phone is enough for a couple or a group: cards are read aloud in turns.
